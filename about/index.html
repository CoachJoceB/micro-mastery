Questions, answered

How does the 7-day trial work?

Start with your invite code and add a payment method. You won't be charged for 7 days. Cancel anytime before day 7 and pay nothing.

What ages is this for?

Worksheets cover upper elementary through high school. Vocabulary spans K–12. Each child works at their own level.

How many kids are covered?

One family plan covers up to 8 children. Each child gets their own profile, reading level, and progress tracking.

Do I need to prep or grade anything?

No. Every worksheet is self-grading with 3 tries per question and instant feedback. Zero parent preparation.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime. Founding members keep their $25/month locked rate if they rejoin later.

What does "locked for life" mean?

Founding members pay $25/month for as long as they stay subscribed. The price never goes up for you, even when the regular price increases.
