Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Who we are

Micro Mastery is a product of Legacy Lab Academy, Inc. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how we protect it. Questions? Email support@mymicromastery.com.

What we collect

  • Account info: your name, email address, and password (stored securely).
  • Family profiles: children's first names or nicknames, grade levels, and reading levels you enter.
  • Learning activity: worksheet answers, scores, and progress so the app can track growth.
  • Payment info: processed by Stripe. We never see or store your card numbers.

Children's data

Micro Mastery is designed for families and stores children's learning data. We collect only what the app needs to function: names or nicknames you provide, grade levels, and worksheet progress. We do not sell children's data, show ads to children, or share children's data with third parties for marketing. Parents can request deletion of their family's data at any time by emailing support@mymicromastery.com.

How we use your data

  • To operate your family account and deliver the service.
  • To send invite codes, receipts, and important service updates.
  • To improve the product (aggregated, anonymized).

We do not sell your data. We do not share it with advertisers.

Data security

Data is stored with Supabase (SOC 2 compliant infrastructure) with row-level security so families can only access their own data. Payments are handled by Stripe (PCI DSS compliant). No system is 100% secure, but we follow industry best practices.

Your rights

You can request a copy of your data, correct it, or delete your family's account and all associated data at any time by emailing support@mymicromastery.com. We respond within 30 days.

Cookies

We use browser local storage to keep you signed in and remember your preferences. No third-party tracking cookies.
