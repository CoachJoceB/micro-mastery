Life Skills

Financial Literacy

~5 min
Interest is extra money a bank pays you for keeping your savings with them. If you put $100 in a savings account that pays 5% interest per year, after one year you'll have $105 — the bank pays you $5 just for keeping your money there. That's your money making money.
Word: Interest — extra money earned on savings.

Science Worksheet

~7 min
Cells are the building blocks of all living things. A typical cell has three main parts: the cell membrane (the outer boundary that controls what goes in and out), the cytoplasm (the jelly-like filling where activity happens), and the nucleus (the control center that holds DNA).
