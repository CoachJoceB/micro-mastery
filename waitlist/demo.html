📖 Reading

Spain's rulers, Ferdinand and Isabella, paid for Christopher Columbus's voyage. He wanted to sail west to find a new sea route to Asia — Europeans were desperate to avoid the dangerous, expensive land crossings and heavy tolls on the old spice routes. In 1492, Columbus landed on an island he named San Salvador, thinking he had reached Asia. Because of his discoveries, Europe found out about America.
